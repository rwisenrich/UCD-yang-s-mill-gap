# Jaffe-Witten v6 closure map

The v4 eight-gate frontier has been attacked one gate at a time. v5 proved the compactness, reflection-positivity, spectral and group-parametric reductions. v6 removes two additional independent quantitative burdens: a separate regulator Cauchy estimate is not required for existence once regulator-uniform Schwartz seminorm bounds are available, and a separate post-limit Hamiltonian-gap estimate is not required once the Euclidean correlators carry a uniform exponential rate.

## Exact closure theorems added in v6

1. Uniform Schwartz seminorm bounds imply subsequential compactness in `S'` by Alaoglu-Bourbaki.
2. Uniform Euclidean exponential decay implies spectral exclusion below the same rate.
3. A positive gap is finite in any nontrivial theory with one-dimensional vacuum kernel.
4. A Bakry-Emery terminal margin survives a `C2` perturbation with Hessian defect smaller than the base margin.
5. Dense-group invariance plus distributional continuity implies full symmetry.
6. OS algebraic/positivity properties are closed under the controlled distributional limit.
7. Balaban-type `g^p`, `p>2`, localized UV remainders are summable over infinitely many AF scales.
8. Uniform source analyticity gives all factorial connected moment bounds by multivariable Cauchy estimates.
9. An `a^alpha` anisotropy insertion gives `O(a^alpha)` covariance restoration under a uniform covariance bound.
10. A uniform negative drift of `q=g^{-2}` reaches any fixed strong-coupling threshold in a finite, explicitly bounded number of RG steps.

## Final atomic frontier

The proof graph now has two quantitative nodes.

### A1 — nonperturbative RG corridor

Prove for the exact compact-group Wilson/heat-kernel RG, with constants independent of UV depth and volume,

`q_(k+1) <= q_k - c_G` on the compact intermediate interval and sufficient localized-remainder bounds to enter a certified massive terminal basin.

### A2 — source-extended RG uniformity

Prove a regulator/volume-independent source polydisc for arbitrary finite families of gauge-invariant curvature composites and metric/anisotropy insertions, with a uniform bound on `log Z(J)` and AF-summable source remainders. This single estimate yields local fields, tempered limits, O(4) restoration, the stress tensor, OPE control and nontriviality.

The dependency theorem in the paper proves `A1 + A2 => Jaffe-Witten existence and mass gap` for each fixed compact simple group.
